🥬 Dipping Greens

Daisy talks about dipping vegetables in salt water.

The green vegetable means spring is here. The salt water is for tears.

Dip your vegetable in the salt water and eat it!

🫓 Breaking the Matzah

Moto breaks the matzah in half and hides one piece.

That hidden piece is called the afikomen. You get to find it after dinner!

🌟 Your Part! The Four Questions

Your Part!

📖 The Story

David and Monica tell the story. Listen along!

A long time ago, our people were slaves in Egypt. A man named Moses said, 'Let my people go!' And after a lot of trouble, Pharaoh finally said okay.

They left so fast their bread didn't have time to rise. That's why we eat flat matzah!

🐸 Ten Plagues

Zoe talks about the ten plagues. Spill a drop of juice for each one!

Everyone says: Blood. Frogs. Lice. Flies. Cattle disease. Boils. Hail. Locusts. Darkness. Death of the firstborn.

We spill a drop because even when we are happy, we feel sad that other people had to suffer.

🎵 Dayenu!

Tai tells us about the Dayenu song. Then everyone sings!

Dayenu means 'it would have been enough.' It's about being thankful.

Everyone says: Da-da-yenu, da-da-yenu, da-da-yenu, dayenu dayenu!

Everyone says: Da-da-yenu, da-da-yenu, da-da-yenu, dayenu dayenu!

🍽️ Three Special Foods

Ryan talks about three important things on the seder plate.

The bone reminds us of long ago. The matzah is flat bread. The bitter herb (horseradish) is spicy!

Everyone takes a sip from their cup.

🥪 Time to Eat the Special Foods!

Moto tells everyone what to eat.

First: eat a piece of matzah. Then: try the horseradish with the brown stuff (charoset). Then: make a sandwich!

🎉 Dinner Time!

Time to eat real food! After dinner, you get to find the hidden matzah (the afikomen)!

🔍 After Dinner

We open the door for a visitor named Elijah. He's invisible but very welcome.

Daisy talks about Miriam, who was Moses's big sister.
